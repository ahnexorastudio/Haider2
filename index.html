01 Start with understanding

Knowledge makes
room for change.

What is addiction?

Drug addiction is a condition that creates a strong urge to use addictive substances despite harmful consequences.

It can affect a person’s health, behaviour, relationships and future.

Listen without judgement

02 The causes

It can start
with a moment.

Many pressures can make substance use more likely. Understanding them helps us respond with care.

  • 01Peer pressureThe influence of friends or a social group.
  • 02Curiosity & stressPersonal problems and difficult moments.
  • 03Lack of awarenessNegative social influences can encourage use.

03 The impact

The effects reach
beyond one person.

Addiction can affect health, relationships, education and the opportunities ahead.

01 / 02

Health

Mind and body
need care.

Drug addiction can damage physical health, memory, concentration and decision-making. It may also worsen mental health and reduce motivation.

02 / 02

Society

Every connection
matters.

Addiction can affect education, family relationships, friendships and career opportunities. It can also create emotional and financial stress for families and communities.

04 A way forward

Real support.

Prevention starts with awareness. Recovery deserves patience, professional care and people who stay.
